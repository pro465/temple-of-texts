l/left -- turn left.
r/right -- turn right.
m/move -- move into the room behind the door in front of you.
s/show -- show what's before you.
k/key -- enter the key to escape.

q/quit -- quit this game.

dump -- dump current state in the form of a code.
load -- load the state from code and resume the game.

<anything else> -- show this message.
